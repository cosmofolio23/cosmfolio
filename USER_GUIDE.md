# CosmoFolio User Guide
## AI-Powered Architecture Portfolio Generator

---

## Welcome to CosmoFolio

CosmoFolio turns your architectural projects into stunning, shareable portfolios in minutes. Upload your drawings, renders, and plans — our AI writes the descriptions, selects the layout, and generates a professional export ready to share with clients or employers.

---

## Quick Start (5 minutes)

### Step 1 — Create Account
1. Visit [cosmofolio.com](https://cosmofolio.com)
2. Click **Sign Up**
3. Enter your email and password
4. Verify your email

### Step 2 — Create Your First Portfolio
1. Click **+ New Portfolio** on the dashboard
2. Enter a title (e.g. "Residential Tower 2026")
3. Add a brief description
4. Click **Create**

### Step 3 — Upload Assets
1. Drag-and-drop images into the **Assets** panel
2. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, SVG, PDF
3. Add labels: Floor Plan, Section, Render, Diagram

### Step 4 — Generate AI Content
1. Click **Generate with AI**
2. Choose a tone: *Professional*, *Academic*, or *Creative*
3. Review the generated descriptions and edit as needed

### Step 5 — Choose a Style
1. Open the **Style** tab
2. Select one of 7 design packs:
   - **Minimal White** — clean, academic
   - **Dark Studio** — bold, gold accents
   - **Scandinavian** — warm Nordic tones
   - **Architectural Journal** — editorial magazine style
   - **Competition Board** — high-contrast poster format
   - **Parametric** — monospace, neon green
   - **Corporate** — professional blue accent
3. Preview updates live in the viewport

### Step 6 — Export or Publish
- **Export PDF** — download A4/A3/Letter document
- **Export HTML** — interactive web version
- **Export ZIP** — full package with all assets
- **Publish** — get a public shareable link

## Feature Reference

### Portfolio Editor

| Feature | How to Access |
|---------|--------------|

4. **Plan + Section + Render** — technical three-panel
5. **Diagram Heavy** — four-column diagram layout
6. **Competition Board** — poster-style A1 format
7. **Timeline** — vertical project evolution
8. **Masonry** — Pinterest-style variable heights
9. **List View** — vertical list with thumbnails
10. **Carousel** — horizontal scrolling gallery
11. **Collage** — artistic freeform arrangement
12. **Custom** — define your own grid

### AI Content Generation

The AI assistant (powered by Llama 2 / Mistral) can write:
- Project descriptions
- Section captions
- Technical summaries
- Keywords & tags
- Portfolio bio

**Tone options:** Professional · Academic · Creative · Minimalist

**Rate limit:** 5 AI generations per minute

## Sharing Your Portfolio

### Public Link
1. Open your portfolio → click **Share**
2. Go to the **Link** tab
3. Click **Copy** to copy the public URL
4. Share `cosmofolio.com/p/your-slug/token`

### Password Protection
1. In Share → Link tab, toggle **Password Protect**
2. Set a password
3. Share the link — viewers must enter the password

### Social Media
1. Share tab → **Social** tab
2. Customise your message
3. Click platform button (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, Email)

### QR Code
- Share tab → **Link** tab → QR code displayed automatically
- Click **Download QR Code** for a PNG

### Temporary Share Token
- Share tab → **Link** tab → **Create Share Link**
- Expires in 30 days by default (configurable)

## Analytics

Track who views your portfolio:

1. Share tab → **Analytics** tab
2. See:
   - Total views (last 30 days)
   - Unique visitors
   - Top traffic source
   - Device breakdown (desktop / mobile / tablet)
3. Click **Refresh** for live updates

## Troubleshooting

**Images not uploading**
- Check file size (max 20 MB per image)
- Supported: JPG, PNG, SVG, PDF
- Try a different browser

**AI generation not responding**
- Check your internet connection
- You may have hit the rate limit (5/min) — wait 60 seconds
- If the issue persists, refresh the page

**PDF export is blank**
- Ensure at least one asset has been added
- Try a different style pack
- Check the browser console for errors

**Public link returns 404**
- The portfolio may have been unpublished
- The share token may have expired (30-day limit)
- Contact support if the issue persists
