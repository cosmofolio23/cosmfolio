## 2. Most Common Layout Patterns

### Cover Strategies (ranked by frequency)
1. **Full-bleed hero image with overlaid title** (used in ~30% of templates) — most architect-friendly, photo-led
2. **Oversized typography dominant, image secondary** (~20%) — brutalist, manifesto, editorial
3. **Color block geometric covers** (~15%) — bold modern, young firms
4. **Grid of image fragments** (~10%) — Urban Grid, Bauhaus, Competition Board
5. **Diagram-as-cover** (~10%) — Plan Library, Section Cut, Diagram Architect
6. **Classical bordered/framed** (~10%) — Heritage, Vintage, Sacred
7. **Sketch / hand-drawn covers** (~5%) — Sketchbook Process, Sketches & Concepts

### Project Page Patterns
1. **Image grid (uniform 2x2, 2x3, 3x3)** — 35%
2. **Hero image + caption + technical drawings stack** — 25%
3. **Before/After split pages** — 8% (Adaptive Reuse)
4. **Full-bleed photo journal** — 12%
5. **Competition board (one project per page, dense)** — 5%
6. **Editorial multi-column with pull quotes** — 10%
7. **Section/elevation foldout focus** — 5%

### About Page Patterns
1. Portrait + bio in 2-column asymmetric (most common)
2. Manifesto-only (no portrait)
3. Studio team photo + credentials grid
4. Timeline of career/work
5. Map of project locations
6. Geometric/decorative portrait composition

## 4. Typography Patterns

### Most Used Heading Fonts (across all templates)
1. **Inter** — 11 templates — modern, tech, technical, workspace
2. **Cormorant Garamond / Cormorant** — 9 templates — elegant, residential, heritage
3. **Lora** — 6 templates — narrative, warm, residential
4. **Source Serif Pro** — 5 templates — academic, civic, healthcare
5. **Playfair Display** — 4 templates — editorial, cultural
6. **Space Grotesk** — 4 templates — futurist, parametric, modular
7. **Oswald** — 3 templates — industrial, sports
8. **Bebas Neue** — 2 templates — concrete, cinematic

### Body Fonts
1. **Inter** — most common (22 templates)
2. **Source Serif Pro / Source Sans Pro** — 8 templates
3. **Karla** — 4 templates
4. **Roboto** — 4 templates
5. **EB Garamond** — 3 templates (classical)

### Pattern Insights
- **Serif headings + sans body** is the prevailing combo (60% of templates)
- **Monospace accent** (JetBrains Mono, Roboto Mono, IBM Plex Mono) used in 12 templates for technical/captions
- **All-sans** is preferred for modern/tech templates
- **All-serif** preferred for editorial/heritage/storyteller

## 5. Content Placeholder Patterns

### Average Content Needs per Template
- **Renders:** 4.3 per template (range: 2–12)
- **Plans:** 2.2 per template (range: 0–6)
- **Sections:** 1.7 per template (range: 0–6)
- **Diagrams:** 2.8 per template (range: 0–10)
- **Text description:** Universal (all 60)

### Content-Heavy vs. Content-Light Templates
- **Heaviest:** Plan Library, Section Cut Master, Academic Research, Urban Diagram (require 15+ content pieces)
- **Lightest:** Mono Sans, Nordic Minimal, Photo Journal, Black Canvas (3–8 content pieces)

### Specialized Metadata Used
- `materials` — 9 templates (industrial, residential, heritage)
- `client` — 8 templates (commercial)
- `scope` / `gfa` / `sqft` — 12 templates (technical, commercial)
- `concept_statement` / `thesis` — 8 templates (academic, conceptual)
- `before_after_pairs` — 1 template (Adaptive Reuse)
- `sustainability_metrics` / `certifications` — 4 templates (sustainable)
- `manifesto_statement` — 2 templates (conceptual)
- `awards` — 4 templates (premium)
- `historical_context` — 5 templates (heritage)

## 9. Implementation Notes for Frontend

### Filtering / Search Schema
Templates should be filterable by:
- **Category** (Residential, Commercial, Conceptual, Heritage, etc.)
- **Style** (Minimal, Bold, Classical, Technical)
- **Color mood** (Light, Dark, Warm, Cool, Vibrant)
- **Content density** (Quick, Standard, Detailed)
- **Format** (Portrait, Landscape, A3)
- **Page count** (Short, Medium, Long)

### Sort Options
- Most popular
- Newest
- Alphabetical
- By page count
- By category

### Preview Quality
Each template needs:
1. A primary preview image (cover representation)
2. 2–3 spread previews (project page, about page)
3. Color swatch chips visible in preview
4. Font sample line visible in preview
